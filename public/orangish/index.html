For sports clubs and facilities

Your whole club on one system.

Orangish runs Exton Sports Center, a 24/7 multi-sport club in Chester County: courts and lanes, payments, packs and classes, the club phone, and the screens on the wall. It also runs two squash clubs in New Jersey and Connecticut. Every screen on this page is live at Exton.

Booking that fills courts

Members book from their phone in seconds. Visitors book and pay without making an account. You set the prices, the hours and the rules.

  • Courts, lanes and whole halls. One court, several courts, or a whole hall for a team game or a party.
  • 1 to 3 hours, in half-hour steps, with peak and off-peak prices shown before anyone pays.
  • No account needed for visitors. They pick a sport and a time, answer your questions, and pay by card.
  • Season bookings for academies and teams, held every week and billed to their account.
  • Players manage their own bookings: move or cancel within your policy, and invite friends with one link.
  • Confirmations and reminders by email and text, the day before and two hours before.
  • Online waivers signed on the phone before players arrive.

Packs, classes and juniors

Sell more than single bookings: prepaid hours, class bundles and beginner programmes, each with its own simple page.

  • Court packs. Prepaid court hours at a saving, with promo codes when you want to run an offer.
  • Classes and squads with online sign-up, drop-in payment, share links and class lists for the instructor.
  • Class and lesson packs, with automatic discounts for partner groups.
  • A beginner Starter Pack parents can buy in minutes.
  • Family accounts and junior safety. Parents act for their children, and children's names never show in public.

Payments, straight to your bank

Your club is the merchant. Money lands in your own bank account through Stripe, and the books keep themselves.

  • Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay. You choose whether the club or the player covers card fees.
  • Memberships that renew themselves, monthly or annual. Members update their own card.
  • A "pay the club" page and payment links for anything that isn't a booking.
  • One ledger for every sale, refund and tax amount, with revenue reports.
  • Checked against Stripe every night, so nothing goes missing.
  • A pro shop till at the desk, and scan-to-pay for members.

Talk to your members

Reminders, newsletters and your own club phone line, all in one place.

  • Newsletters and a mailing list. Collect sign-ups on your website, import past members, and send branded emails like this one.
  • Your own club phone line with a short call menu that rings your staff and screens robocalls. Calls are recorded and summarised.
  • One inbox for calls and texts, with instant alerts. Reply or call back from the club number.
  • In-app chat and announcements for members.
